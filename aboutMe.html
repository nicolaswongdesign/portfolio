ABOUT ME

In 2016, Nicolas pursued design as his career, making his mark in the field. By 2020, he was actively engaged in design work.

📖 My Backstories

I was originally born in Indonesia, where I spent my first 12 years. Moving to the US was a significant challenge for me, especially since I had never really spoken English before. One thing I miss the most is the food they have there. Despite encountering many unfamiliar things, I learned to adapt and embrace them.

🎨 My Hobbies

I do things beyond designing, and it might be hard to believe, but it's true. This includes gaming, content creation, and traveling. One of my hobbies that I recently picked up is cooking. I not only learned it from the people around me, but also influenced by the delicious food I ate throughout my life. I am always inspired to make yummy food to feed the people around me.

🛣 My Future Path

Design always have been my passion and it is always something I look forward to. Recently, I am more focused on finding the "why" of designing, learning about the audience and how they think. This puts me into a deeper understanding of the purpose of designing and a great approach to success.
